\subsection{QA Reflection}

Snapshot 3 testing highlighted the complexity of cross-platform notification systems and scheduled background processing. The team learned that notification timing and queue management required significantly more testing than originally expected.

Testing also reinforced the need for stress testing and device-specific validation when implementing background services.

\subsection{Final Assessment}

Snapshot 3 objectives were completed successfully, with notification systems and advisory features functioning reliably across supported platforms. Most critical defects were resolved before the conclusion of QA testing.
